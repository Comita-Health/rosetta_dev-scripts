# Comita brand spelling

The company and the apps are spelled **Comita**. **Kamita** is the
pronunciation only — never the spelling in UI, git, issues, PRDs, ADRs,
specs, or code identifiers.

## Use

- **Comita** — product name, in-app copy, docs, commits
- **Comita says** / **Run Comita** / **Comita Therapy** — UI and PRD
  wording
- `run_comita` — identifiers (not `run_kamita`)

## Do not

- Spell the product **Kamita** in user-visible copy or source
- Treat “Kamita” as an alternate brand or a rejected old name

When the pronunciation must be documented, say: spelled **Comita**,
pronounced “Kamita.”
